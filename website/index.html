Get back on the road. Build a Switch 2 package from your own Xbox 360 game, right here in your browser.

USA EDITIONNO GAME UPLOADSv1.0.0
Before you start

You need your own USA Xbox 360 copy and the Switch 2 release NRO. Your files never leave your computer. The port is experimental; stable 60 FPS and the release build are not yet verified.

01

Choose your game

Select your disc image or the extracted folder containing default.xex, NFS and Movies.

No game selected.

02

Choose the Switch 2 NRO

Download the USA Switch 2 NRO, then select it here. It will be checked against the release fingerprint.

No NRO selected.

03

Create your package

The installer generates and checks the shaders, then saves a ZIP. Use a current desktop Chrome or Edge; several GB of free space may be needed.

04

Copy to your Switch 2

Extract the ZIP into sdmc:/switch/. Launch the homebrew menu using title takeover (hold R while opening a game), then start nfsmw-nx.
